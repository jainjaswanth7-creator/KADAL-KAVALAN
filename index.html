Android sensor connection

For reliable motion access, open this app in Chrome from an HTTPS website (not file:// or content://). The app will attempt the sensor even when the page is not secure, but Chrome may refuse readings. On Android open Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions and check the relevant permissions; in Chrome check Settings → Site settings → Motion sensors if your version offers it. Keep the phone screen awake and the page visible.

Guaranteed twelve-tap demonstration (no motion hardware needed)

Tap +1 DEMO STEP twelve times. The on-screen step count increases and the alarm starts on the twelfth tap. This works as the reliable fallback, even when the browser does not expose physical motion sensors.

For school: Start walking sensor on a secure HTTPS site and allow motion access. Hold your phone in your hand while walking. If it does not work, use +1 metre twelve times and +0.802 m once. Downloaded local HTML files often cannot access sensors. GPS typically cannot measure 12.802 m reliably indoors. Keep the app in the foreground with volume on. Android may vibrate; iPhone web browsers often cannot. This never measures a real sea boundary.
